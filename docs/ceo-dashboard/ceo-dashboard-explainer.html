CEO Overview Explained

Every card on the How the business is doing page, in plain words: what it shows, which table it reads, how the number is worked out, an example, and what to watch out for.

How to read this

Each card below has a small strip that shows the journey of its number from left to right. The colours always mean the same thing.

table in the database rule that filters rows calculation what the card shows

For the full end-to-end map of tables, server modules and screen sections, open the companion diagram: ceo-overview-dataflow.html (same folder; it supports zoom, search and export).

Three facts that apply to the whole page

  • Invoices come from two systems. Zoho invoices and the Drive (Vyapar) invoices are joined into one list. Cancelled (void) invoices are dropped everywhere. Draft invoices count as revenue but never as money owed. Credit notes count as negative revenue on the day they are issued.
  • Days are Indian days. Most date checks convert timestamps to Asia/Kolkata before deciding which day a thing happened on.
  • Some cards follow the period, some do not. "As of now" cards (Needs you today, Money owed, Dealer base, Trust checks) ignore the period chips. Each card below says which kind it is.

Period control

top right of the page

The chips This month · Last month · Quarter · Financial year and the from–to date boxes choose one window. That window is sent to every API that follows the period.

This month
From the 1st of this month to today.
Last month
The whole of last month, 1st to last day.
Quarter
From the first day of the current quarter to today.
Financial year
From 1 April to today.
Custom dates
Exactly the days you pick. If typed backwards they are swapped. The "to" date cannot be in the future.
Previous period
Count the days in the window, then take the same number of days immediately before it. "1–6 Oct" is compared with "25–30 Sep".
As of HH:MM
The server time when the page last fetched. The figures refresh every 60 seconds while the page is open.
Watch out. The subtitle says "each figure compares with the previous period", but only two figures actually show a comparison: Revenue and the buyback kg sourced. All other tiles show "No target" or a share instead.

Needs you today

row of action cards · as of now · ignores the period

Only cards with a number above zero are drawn. Cards at zero are listed in the green "All clear" line underneath. When everything is zero the row says "Nothing needs you right now."

Quotes waiting for your approval bad

A rep cannot send a quote above the automatic limit until the CEO approves it. This counts those quotes sitting in the queue right now.

Sales-ready leads with no owner warn

A lead is sales-ready when a dated event says so: a rep created it, a rep claimed it, the AI call qualified it, or a NeoDove agent picked it up. If such a lead has nobody assigned, it waits here. The waiting clock starts at that event, not at the day the lead was created.

Example. 20 leads are sales-ready with no owner. 15 of them became sales-ready more than 7 days ago. The card shows 20 and the line "15 waiting more than 7 days". "Open list" opens the Ready to assign page.

Leads idle over 7 working days warn

"Last work" is the latest of: the last logged touchpoint, the assignment date, or the creation date. Sundays are skipped. Public holidays are not skipped here.

Dealers in Red or Dormant bad

These are dealers who used to order and have gone quiet. See the Dealer base card for all the buckets.

People below 80% of target bad

For each person, every metric's "% of target to date" is averaged. Anyone whose average is under 80% is counted. If no targets are set up at all, the card cannot be counted and a note with a link to Targets appears instead.

NBFCs waiting for your approval warn

Watch out. "Not tracked yet: dealer said yes but not marked Won · orders claimed without an invoice" is fixed text. Neither of those two things is counted anywhere yet.

KPI tiles

six tiles · most follow the period

Revenue

Counted
Every invoice whose invoice date falls in the window, from both Zoho and Drive, unless it is void. Draft invoices are counted. A credit note subtracts its amount on its issue date.
Comparison
The same sum over the previous window of the same length. Shown as ▲ or ▼ with the percentage change. Hidden when the previous value is zero.
Small text
"₹X not linked to a dealer" appears when some invoices could not be matched to any dealer account or lead (usually a missing GSTIN).
Example. Window 1–6 Oct has invoices of ₹4,20,000 and ₹1,80,000 plus a credit note of ₹20,000. Revenue = ₹5,80,000. The previous window 25–30 Sep had ₹2,90,000, so the tile shows ▲ 100% vs previous period.

Gross margin

Unit cost
First choice: the average OEM purchase price of that product bought in the 180 days before the sale. Second: the average over all costed stock of that product. Third: the OEM reference price in force on the sale date.
Not costed
A line whose product cannot be matched, or has no known cost, is left out of the margin and reported as "not costed". The tool never guesses a cost.
The pill
Margin divided by the revenue of the costed lines only, not by total revenue.
Not included
Zoho invoices and credit notes. The dash "—" means no costed lines in the period.

Batteries to dealers

Battery lines on invoices dated in the window, matched to a dealer account or lead. On a database without the invoice-line tables it falls back to counting stock units allocated to dealers.

Watch out. The period for this tile is capped at 366 days. A longer custom range shows "—".

New dealers live

A dealer goes live when admin approves the onboarding. The small text counts leads that reached Converted in the same window; the two usually match.

Dealers ordering as of today

Ignores the period. "Live" is every dealer in every bucket, including those who never ordered.

Money owed to us as of now

Zoho side
Zoho tracks payments, so the balance it sends is reliable.
Drive side
The import only reads the invoice PDF. It cannot know whether the dealer paid. Balance = total minus whatever a finance user has typed in as paid. Until someone records a payment, every Drive invoice counts as fully unpaid.
Credit notes
Carry no balance, so they do not reduce this figure.
Example from sandbox. Zoho shows 3 overdue invoices worth ₹43,954. Drive holds 67 invoices worth ₹1.97 Cr with no payment recorded. The tile shows ₹1.97 Cr. Recording payments on the CEO invoices page brings it down.

Revenue pace

line chart · follows the period
  1. Add up revenue per day (or per month for long windows), then draw the running total.
  2. If the window is still running, divide the revenue so far by the days already passed to get a daily rate.
  3. Multiply that rate by the total days in the window. That is the dotted "at current pace" end point.
Example. Six days into October the running total is ₹5,80,000. Daily rate ≈ ₹96,667. October has 31 days, so the pace line ends near ₹30 L.
Watch out. There is no company revenue target, so no target line is drawn. A custom range that does not start on the 1st, or that spans two months, plots the points in the wrong place on the x-axis.

Where revenue came from

bars by business type or by city · follows the period

Each invoice is matched to a dealer through its GSTIN (or a hand link made on the invoices page). The dealer's lead gives the business type and city.

Watch out. The city view only knows cities from leads. An invoice matched to a dealer account that has no lead disappears from the city bars, and the list is capped at the top 10 cities. So city bars can add up to less than the Revenue tile.

Money in, money out

two cards · whole months only

Revenue against total costs, last 6 months

This chart always shows the last six months and does not follow the period chips. "Stock bought" is the amount on OEM purchase invoices dated in that month. "Running expenses" are approved expense claims by expense date.

Where the money went, period

The same three figures summed for the months in the chosen window, plus a per-department split of expenses when "This month" is selected. The bottom line "Net of purchases" is sales minus stock bought minus expenses.

Watch out. Costs exist per whole month only. A custom range that is not "1st to month-end" shows a hint instead of figures. This card counts stock bought, not stock sold, so a month of heavy stocking looks negative.

Sales engine

funnel rows · follows the period
  1. Take every lead created inside the window. That is the cohort, shown as "All leads created".
  2. For each stage, count how many of those leads are at that stage now or ever passed through it, using the status history.
  3. The bar is that count as a share of the cohort.
Headline conversion
Once the CRM has more than 120 days of leads it shows "30-day lead conversion": of leads created 31–60 days ago, the share that converted within 30 days. This ignores the chosen period on purpose, so it is comparable month to month.
AI dialler line
"N dealers called": leads with an AI call in the window. "p% connected": those that connected. "q qualified": those whose current AI band is Qualified.
First orders
Leads whose very first invoice is dated in the window.

Can you trust these numbers?

six checks · as of now

Each check measures the share of records that are missing something. 0% is the good end. Every other number on the page is under-counted until these reach zero.

Team against target

one row per person · follows the period
% of target to date
Actual ÷ (monthly target × working days passed ÷ working days in the month) × 100, averaged across the person's metrics. The black tick is 100%. Green ≥ 100, amber ≥ 80, red below.
Revenue
Invoices in the window credited to the person who owned the dealer on the invoice date.
Converted
Leads that reached Converted in the window, credited to the closing owner (who held the lead when it was won), never to whoever holds it now.
Idle leads
Their leads with no work for 7+ working days, as of now. Bold red at 15 or more.
Engaged
Of the person's human calls in the window, the share that connected. Duplicate NeoDove entries within 3 minutes count once. Duration does not matter.
Watch out. "% of target" always uses the targets of the month in which the window ends. For Quarter and Financial year the revenue and converted columns cover the whole window, but the target column is one month only.

Customer finance (NBFC)

three counts · follows the period

KYC files shared counts customers whose KYC first entered the verification queue in the window. Disbursed counts loans with a disbursement date in the window. Rejected counts sanctions in rejected status, dated by their last update because there is no separate rejection date.

Battery buyback

three figures · follows the period
Sourced
Total weight of every line on requests whose pickup was completed in the window. Shown in tonnes above 1,000 kg. Compared with the previous window.
Deals closed
Requests submitted in the window that have a settled or closed deal. Note it is dated by submission, not by the close.
Paid per kg
Money paid to dealers in the window divided by the kg above.

Green Energy Today

news brief · as of now

Today's AI-written brief of green-energy news with its sources, plus the latest six items from the last 24 hours. It refreshes every two hours and the brief is written on the first refresh after 06:00 IST. Visible to the CEO role only.

Known gaps, as of 6 Oct 2026

Things the code does today that the labels do not say, or that may surprise a reader.

  1. The pace chart mis-plots a custom range that does not start on the 1st or that crosses a month boundary.
  2. "Where the money went" shows ₹0 for a whole-month range older than six months instead of saying it is not available.
  3. "Batteries to dealers" small text says "allocated"; the figure is actually invoiced battery quantity.
  4. "Gross margin" small text says "whole months"; the figure uses exact days.
  5. City bars ignore invoices matched to an account without a lead, and show only the top 10 cities.
  6. "People below 80%" picks the month by UTC, so for a few hours after midnight on the 1st it can look at the wrong month. When targets are missing it wrongly appears in the "All clear" line.
  7. Team "% of target" is one month while revenue and converted span the whole period for Quarter and Financial year.
  8. The sales-engine headline conversion ignores the chosen period once the CRM is older than 120 days. This is by design but is not stated on screen.
  9. The subtitle promises a comparison for every figure; only Revenue and buyback kg have one.
  10. Buyback "deals closed" is dated by submission, not by the close.
  11. Money owed treats every Drive invoice as unpaid until a payment is recorded by hand.

Glossary

Void

A cancelled invoice. Dropped from every figure.

Draft

An invoice not yet sent. Counted as revenue, never as money owed.

Credit note

A refund document. Subtracts from revenue on its issue date.

Matched invoice

An invoice linked to a dealer by GSTIN or by a manual link.

Sales-ready

A lead that a rep, the AI call or a NeoDove agent has shown is worth working.

Closing owner

Whoever held a lead when it was marked Won. Keeps the credit forever.

Engaged call

A human call that connected and the rep spoke with the dealer. Length does not matter.

Working day

Monday to Saturday. Sundays are skipped; holidays only where the targets rules say so.

Window

The from–to range chosen with the chips or dates.

Previous period

The same number of days immediately before the window.
